WinLauncher Standard public sample CSV files

On the Launcher tab, select Launcher_Sample_EN.csv from Import CSV. On the Snippets tab, select Snippets_Sample_EN.csv.

Notes:
- Samples are not imported automatically.
- The fictional paths under C:\WinLauncherSample will not open unless you create them.
- Replace sample paths and URLs with your own as needed.
- Imported rows are added to your existing data, so back up first if necessary.
